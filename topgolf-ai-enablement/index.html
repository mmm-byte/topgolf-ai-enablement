How the design treats guests

Principles

Offers that respect time

Frequency caps are enforced in the system, not left to campaign managers. A guest who has heard from us twice this week hears nothing more until next week.

No dark patterns

Cancelling a membership or opting out takes the same number of taps as signing up. Urgency labels such as "last chance" require a real deadline.

Families come first

Topgolf venues serve families and young guests. Nothing in this platform targets minors, and any campaign that might reach them goes through an additional review.
