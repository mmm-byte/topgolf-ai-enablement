Campaign studio

Build a campaign brief from an audience, an offer and a goal. The checks run on the brief before anyone sends it, and they decide who has to approve it. Audience sizes are synthetic.

    How the checks work
    Rules, written out
    Each rule is a plain statement that a reviewer can read and challenge. Rules are versioned with the platform.
    
      
    
        
    Blocks the send
    • ×Urgency words ("last chance", "ends", "today only") with no real end date.
    • ×Text or email without an opt-out.
    • ×Guests in the audience already at the two-message weekly cap.
    
        
    Routes to an approver
    • !Superlatives or guarantees ("best", "guaranteed", "lowest") go to legal.
    • !Comparisons with another venue go to legal.
    • !Family and youth audiences go to the youth review group, whatever the goal.
